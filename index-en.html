00 / CompanyWho we are

We manufacture in Mexico. We design the software that manufacturing runs on.

Stonward Dynamics develops design and manufacturing software, and builds the platforms that software controls.

Our line includes polar, delta and CoreXY 3D printers, a six-axis articulated robot, a five-axis machining centre and a CNC lathe. Every machine is designed, simulated and validated with our own tools before it is built. In parallel we research robotics, bionics, life-science instrumentation and artificial intelligence applied to engineering.

01 / SoftwareSolidMaker · Open beta

SolidMaker. Design, simulation and manufacturing in one environment.

An open engineering platform for students, makers and teams with big ideas who want to build them.

Parametric design, assembly, bench testing and machine control live in the same program and share the same model. What you draw is exactly what gets simulated and what gets made.

03 / PrototypesIn active development
P-01Validation

Closed-loop continuous theta

Position control for the PolarONE rotary axis with an AS5600 absolute magnetic encoder, a PI controller and Kalman-filter estimation on the 6:1 planetary reducer. The disc never stops; error is corrected on the move.

P-02Integration

Multimodal command bridge

A layer that turns voice, gestures and motion into design and machine operations inside SolidMaker. It is the technical foundation of MIA.

P-03Concept

SolidEngine

A high-precision engineering core built to scale beyond the classroom: calculation, validation and manufacturing to industrial standards.
